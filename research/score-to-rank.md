## Reading notes

- **Papers vary wildly in difficulty.** GATE 2025 CSE was widely considered easy (hence a perfect 100 at AIR 1), while GATE 2024 CSE was hard — AIR 1 took only 77. Never compare raw marks across years; compare *normalized GATE score* or rank instead.
- **AIR 1 always maps to GATE score 1000.** The score formula is anchored on the topper's marks, so a 77 in 2024 and a 100 in 2025 both equal 1000.
- **Rough rule of thumb for CSE** (≈ 1.0–1.1 lakh candidates appear, ~15–17k qualify):
  - AIR ~100 → roughly 60–75 raw marks depending on paper difficulty
  - AIR ~1000 → roughly 42–57 raw marks
  - AIR ~5000 → roughly 30–38 raw marks
  - Qualified (bottom of list, ~AIR 15,000) → the UR cutoff, i.e. 25–33 marks
- **IIT M.Tech admission** typically needs AIR within ~1000–1500 for older IITs in CSE, i.e. roughly 50+ raw marks in a normal-difficulty year.
- Cutoffs shown are the **General/UR qualifying cutoff** for paper code CS. OBC-NCL is ≈ 0.9× and SC/ST/PwD ≈ 0.667× the UR cutoff each year.
