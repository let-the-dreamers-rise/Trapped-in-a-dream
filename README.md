# 🎯 GATE Rank 1 — 90 Day Mission

A personal, offline-first, mobile-friendly study app for cracking **GATE CS&IT** in 90 days.

## What's inside

- **Full syllabus, every topic** — 11 subjects, 65 topics (Engineering Maths, Digital Logic, COA, Programming & DS, Algorithms, TOC, Compiler Design, OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, General Aptitude), each topic with four theory layers: *Intro → Core theory → Deep dive (complete textbook-level reference) → Exam strategy*.
- **1,681-question authored bank** — concept, numerical and PYQ-style questions matching the real GATE 2026 pattern: MCQ, **MSQ** (multi-select) and **NAT** (type the number). Every single question shows a **detailed step-by-step explanation right under it** the moment you answer — you learn by solving.
- **Infinite practice** — when you exhaust a topic's bank, the app (a) resurfaces your weakest/wrong questions via Leitner spaced repetition, and (b) *generates* fresh numeric variants (cache math, scheduling tables, subnetting, TCP cwnd, B+-tree order, probability, aptitude…) forever.
- **90-day battle plan** — rigorous day-by-day objectives with checkboxes: what to read, how many questions to solve, when to mock. Phase 1 → score 50/100 by Day 30. Phase 2 → 70 by Day 60. Phase 3 → 90+ by Day 85.
- **Full mock tests** — 65 questions, 100 marks, 3-hour timer, real GATE marking (negative only on MCQs; MSQ all-or-nothing; NAT tolerance-matched), sampling weighted by real subject weightage, complete post-test review with explanations and topper-style mistake tagging (concept / silly / time trap).
- **Progress tracking** — accuracy per subject/topic, weakest-topic radar, mock-score curve vs targets, daily quota and streak.
- **♓ Pisces (Meena rashi) daily card** — lucky colour/number, best study window, mantra, and a small daily ritual.
- **ADHD-friendly by design** — one thing on screen at a time, big tap targets, instant feedback, streaks, a 12-hour day template broken into short blocks with real breaks.

## How to run it on your phone

The app is 100% static — no server, no build, no account.

1. **Easiest:** enable GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root). Open the URL on your phone → browser menu → **"Add to Home Screen"**. It installs like an app and works fully offline.
2. **Or locally:** `python3 -m http.server` in the repo folder and open `http://localhost:8000`.

All progress is stored on your device (localStorage). Nothing leaves your phone.

## Honest note on "200,000 questions"

Rank 1 doesn't come from question count — it comes from doing every high-quality question **properly** (solve → read explanation → redo mistakes). This app ships a serious curated bank plus unlimited generated variants, which is the same effective coverage with none of the junk. Trust the plan, do the daily quota, and let the spaced repetition grind your weak spots flat.
